Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We've Got Answers

Is laser hair removal painful?

Most patients describe the sensation as a rubber band snapping against the skin. Our devices have built-in cooling tips that minimize discomfort. Topical numbing cream is also available upon request.

How many sessions will I need?

Most areas require 6-8 sessions for optimal results, spaced 4-6 weeks apart. Results vary based on hair color, skin type, and treatment area.

Is laser treatment safe for dark skin?

Yes! Our medical-grade diode laser is safe for all skin types (Fitzpatrick I-VI). We use specific wavelengths that target hair follicles without damaging surrounding skin.

Does tattoo removal leave scars?

When performed correctly by experienced professionals, tattoo removal should not leave scars. Our picosecond laser is designed to shatter ink particles while preserving the surrounding skin.

What's the downtime after laser treatment?

For hair removal and rejuvenation, there's zero downtime. You can return to work immediately. Tattoo removal may have 3-7 days of healing with minor scabbing.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes! We offer package discounts and flexible payment options. Ask us about our treatment packages during your consultation.
