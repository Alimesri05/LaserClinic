Skin Rejuvenation

Reduce fine lines, scars, and sun damage. Stimulate collagen for younger-looking, radiant skin.

Treatment Time

15-30 minutes

Recommended Sessions

3-4 sessions for optimal results

Session Spacing

3-4 weeks between sessions

What is Laser Skin Rejuvenation?

Laser skin rejuvenation uses non-ablative laser energy to heat the deeper layers of your skin without damaging the surface. This stimulates collagen production and cell renewal, resulting in smoother, tighter, more youthful-looking skin with zero downtime.

Conditions We Treat

Fine lines & wrinkles
Sun damage & age spots
Acne scars
Large pores
Uneven skin texture
Rosacea & redness

What to Expect

  • 1
    Consultation
    We analyze your skin and discuss your goals to create a custom treatment plan.
  • 2
    Treatment
    Cooling gel is applied. The laser handpiece glides over your skin. You'll feel a warm pulse.
  • 3
    Immediately After
    Mild redness (like a sunburn) for 1-2 hours. You can wear makeup immediately.
  • 4
    Results
    Collagen builds over 3-6 weeks. Improvement continues for months after your final session.

Before & After Care

Before Treatment

  • Avoid sun exposure for 2 weeks
  • Stop retinoids for 1 week
  • Arrive with clean, makeup-free skin
  • No self-tanner for 2 weeks

After Treatment

  • Use gentle cleanser and moisturizer
  • Apply SPF +50 daily (essential!)
  • Avoid harsh exfoliants for 1 week
  • Stay hydrated for best collagen production
