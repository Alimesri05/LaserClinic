Tattoo Removal

Erase unwanted ink with picosecond laser technology. Fewer sessions, better results for all ink colors.

Treatment Time

10-45 minutes depending on tattoo size

Recommended Sessions

4-10 sessions for full removal

Session Spacing

6-8 weeks between sessions

How Picosecond Laser Works

Our picosecond laser delivers ultra-short pulses (trillionths of a second) to shatter tattoo ink into tiny particles. Your body's immune system naturally removes these particles over time. This advanced technology requires fewer sessions than traditional lasers and works on all ink colors.

What to Expect

  • 1
    Consultation
    We evaluate your tattoo (size, colors, age, ink density) and set realistic expectations.
  • 2
    Treatment
    The laser passes over the tattoo. You'll feel a snapping sensation (like a rubber band).
  • 3
    Immediately After
    The area turns frosty white (normal reaction). Ice packs are applied for comfort.
  • 4
    Healing
    Scabbing forms within 24-48 hours. Do not pick! Fading continues for 6-8 weeks.
