Laser Hair Removal

Permanent hair reduction for all skin types using medical-grade diode laser technology with integrated cooling.

Treatment Time

15-60 minutes depending on area

Recommended Sessions

6-8 sessions for optimal results

Session Spacing

4-6 weeks between sessions

What is Laser Hair Removal?

Laser hair removal is a medical procedure that uses concentrated light to remove unwanted hair. The laser emits light absorbed by the pigment (melanin) in the hair. The light energy converts to heat, damaging the hair follicles that produce hairs. This damage inhibits or delays future hair growth.

How It Works

  • 1
    Consultation
    We assess your skin type, hair color, and medical history to create a personalized plan.
  • 2
    Preparation
    The area is cleaned and shaved. Protective eyewear is provided.
  • 3
    Treatment
    The laser handpiece is applied to the skin. A cooling tip protects the skin surface.
  • 4
    Aftercare
    Apply soothing gel or aloe vera. Avoid sun exposure for 2 weeks.

Before & After Care

Before Treatment

  • Avoid sun exposure for 4 weeks
  • Do not wax or pluck for 6 weeks
  • Shave the area 24 hours before
  • Avoid self-tanner

After Treatment

  • Apply cold compresses for redness
  • Use SPF +50 on treated areas
  • Avoid hot showers for 24 hours
  • No gym/sweating for 24 hours
